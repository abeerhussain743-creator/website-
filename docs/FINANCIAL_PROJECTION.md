# Forge — 12-Month Business Model & Financial Projection

Assumptions are **hypotheses for planning**, not committed forecasts. Segment: metal / component manufacturers in the US (expanding regionally later).

---

## 1. Business model

**Product:** Multi-tenant manufacturing OS (SaaS).  
**Primary wedge:** Operational loop (sales → materials → production → invoice).  
**Monetization:** Tiered subscriptions + implementation + add-ons (plants, AI, API).

### Planned ARPU mix (steady state hypothesis)

| Segment | Share of customers | ARPU / mo |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 35% | $120 |
| Growth | 40% | $399 |
| Professional | 20% | $999 |
| Enterprise | 5% | $2,500 |

**Blended ARPU target:** ~$520 / month by month 12 among paying accounts.

### Services revenue
- Implementation / data migration: $2k–$25k one-time  
- Assume 40% of new Professional+ deals buy implementation in year 1

---

## 2. Go-to-market (year 1)

1. **Founding design partners (months 1–3):** 3–5 metal fabricators, discounted Growth plans, heavy onboarding feedback  
2. **Content + outbound (months 3–8):** BOM shortage, job costing, machine downtime pain  
3. **Partner channel (months 6–12):** fractional CFOs, manufacturing consultants, ERP migrators  
4. **Expansion:** upsell QC/warehouse/AI modules; second plant add-on

Year 1 is investment-heavy: revenue ~$164k vs opex ~$433k ⇒ **planned burn ~$270k** unless costs are kept founder-lean.

### Lean variant (2 founders, minimal paid ads)
- Opex ~$220–260k  
- Burn ~$60–100k  
- Still reach ~$24k MRR if logo ramp holds

## 7. Funding / runway framing

To execute the full Phase 1→2 product while selling:

- **Bootstrap lean:** founders + design partners; aim for $10k MRR before raising  
- **Seed (optional):** $750k–$1.2M to fund eng + CS through Phase 2/3 and ~$50k MRR

Use of funds if seeded: 60% product, 25% GTM, 15% ops/buffer.

---

## 9. What must be true

1. Wedge buyers feel pain in **material shortages + job costing**, not “need another CRM.”  
2. Onboarding to first production order < 2 weeks with templates for metal fab.  
3. Dashboard + alerts are owner-visible weekly (habit), not only admin configuration.  
4. Pricing stays plant/complexity based so shopfloor workers don’t explode seat costs.
